Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers about orders, shipping, and returns.

Once your order is shipped, you will receive an email notification containing your tracking number and a link to trace your package's delivery status. You can also view order tracking information directly under your My Account page.
We offer a 30-day return policy for all eligible items. Products must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by the receipt or proof of purchase.
We accept major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, and secure regional online payment methods at checkout.
Orders can be modified or canceled within 2 hours of placement. Please contact our support team immediately through our Contact page for assistance.
